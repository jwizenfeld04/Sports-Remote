Notes on the bundles:
- **Sling** offers no CBS in any market, and its ABC/FOX/NBC locals exist only in some markets. That is a major gap for NFL. Sports Extra (~$11) adds NBA TV, MLB Network, NHL Network, ESPNU, SEC Network, ACC Network, FS2 and BTN, plus RedZone on Blue. Secondary sources report the **Day/Week passes were discontinued in early Oct 2026**.
- **Fubo** has never carried WBD channels, so it has no TNT, TBS or truTV. That matters for NHL and MLB games on TNT/TBS. NBCU channels were blacked out on Fubo from Nov 2025 to Jun 2026; they came back with a price increase of about $15/mo. Disney now owns about 70% of the combined Fubo/Hulu Live business (merger closed Oct 29 2025).
- **Pay-TV login:** YouTube TV, Hulu Live, Fubo, DIRECTV and cable subscribers can activate **ESPN Unlimited** in the ESPN app at no extra cost. Fox One also accepts pay-TV logins. The setup screen should treat these as implied services.

## 5. Over-the-air antenna (pseudo-service)

All OTA options get the market's ABC, CBS, FOX and NBC, which covers most NFL games. They also get team broadcast stations for many NBA and NHL teams that moved to free TV in 2025–26 (for example Bucks, Jazz, Suns, Pelicans, Mavericks, Heat, Lightning, Panthers, Predators, Golden Knights, Kraken, Mammoth and Ducks).

## 8. Recent changes and uncertain items

**Recent changes:**
- **ESPN bought NFL Network** (deal closed Jan 31 2026). NFL Network is now in ESPN Unlimited. RedZone digital rights stay with NFL+ Premium.
- **ESPN price increase on Sept 17 2026:** Select $13.99, Unlimited $31.99. MLB.TV is now sold through ESPN.
- **YouTube TV genre plans** launched Feb 2026, including the Sports Plan at $64.99. ESPN Unlimited was integrated on Sept 1 2026.
- **Fubo and Hulu Live merged** under Disney (Oct 2025). The Fubo–NBCU dispute was resolved in June 2026.
- **Peacock price increase on Aug 18 2026.** NBCSN relaunched Nov 2025. MLB Sunday Night Baseball moved to NBC/Peacock and Netflix gained MLB events, both for 2026–28.

**Low-confidence or unverified items:**
- **Sling:** that the day passes really ended, the exact Sports Extra lineup by base plan, whether NBCSN is carried, and whether NBA League Pass is still an add-on.
- **YouTube TV:** whether the Sports Plan includes TNT/TBS, and where NBA TV, MLB Network and NHL Network sit (base vs Sports Plus).
- **Hulu Live:** whether ESPN Unlimited or only Select is included, and what its Sports add-on contains.
- **Fubo Sports plan:** the full lineup.
- **Apple and MLB after 2026**, and whether HBO Max packaging changes after a Paramount–WBD deal.
- **Small RSNs and team apps:** prices and Google TV apps (SportsNet Pittsburgh, Detroit SportsNet, Altitude+, Spectrum SportsNet+, CHSN+).
- **NBA TV carriage** after its move to NBA operation, across the vMVPDs.
